English Ninja

A desert, a jungle, a dungeon, a village at night and islands in the clouds. The creatures hold pictures — a voice tells you in English what to find, and you hit it with your shuriken!

Choose your ninja

What shall we practise?

Walk: arrows / WASD or drag your finger. Throw a shuriken: space (or tap the screen / the ★ button). H — hear the word again, P — pause.

About

English Ninja — a game for learning English words, a sister of Liczbowy Ninja (Number Ninja).
